Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what data Wallpaper AI collects and why.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, and your Google account ID if you sign in with Google. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
  • Images: the photos you upload and the images generated from them.
  • Usage data: your credit balance, generation history and payment records (amount, date, pack).

How we use it

Only to run the Service: to log you in, generate images, show your gallery and keep track of credits. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare — image storage (R2) and AI processing for free previews.
  • OpenRouter / Google — AI processing for premium generations.
  • Neon — database hosting. Railway — application hosting.
  • Polar — payments. We never see or store your card details.
  • Google — sign-in, if you choose "Continue with Google".

Cookies

We use a single essential cookie to keep you logged in. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us to export or delete your account and all your images at any time by writing to rostik.matsko109@gmail.com.
