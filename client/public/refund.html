Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Credits are sold as one-time packs through our Merchant of Record, Creem.

Unused credits

If you have not used any credits from a pack, you can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase.

Used credits

Credits that have already been spent on generations are not refundable, because the AI processing cost has already been incurred. If a generation failed, the credit is returned to your balance automatically.

Technical problems

If something went wrong with a paid generation or a purchase, write to us and we will make it right — usually by restoring credits or refunding the payment.
